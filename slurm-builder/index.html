TWO DIFFERENT JOB SETTINGS

Account vs. QoS

Account decides which university allocation or credit pool is charged.

QoS controls scheduling priority, runtime limits, and preemption policy.

Every user has a general association. Eligible users also have separate course, faculty, RF/PhD, or paid associations. Each account uses only its matching QoS.

Paid, Faculty, RF/PhD, and Course jobs can preempt General jobs after the cluster-wide 10-minute exemption. They do not preempt one another. Preempted jobs are requeued, so checkpoint when practical.
